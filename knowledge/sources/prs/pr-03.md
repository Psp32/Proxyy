## Product Workflow

The updated landing page explains the complete Sustaina workflow:

### Automated Purchase Tracking

Partner stores can send receipt data to Sustaina.

AI extracts information including:

- Product name
- Manufacturer
- Expiry date
- Quantity purchased
- Optional price information

Users can then receive notifications through WhatsApp or Telegram.

### Expiry Tracking

The platform monitors product expiry dates and provides reminders.

Examples include:

- Urgent expiry notifications.
- Weekly summaries of products approaching expiry.

### AI-Powered Consumption Logging

Users can log consumption using voice or text.

For example:

> "I finished the bread."

The system can then remove the consumed item from tracking.

Sustaina can also suggest recipes intended to help users reduce waste.

### Manual Receipt Scanning

Users can upload or scan receipts from stores that are not integrated with Sustaina.

AI-powered OCR extracts product information from the receipt.

### Sustainability Dashboard

The web dashboard provides insights including:

- Waste reduction trends.
- Consumption habits.
- Recycling suggestions.
